**Dataset**: fundamental23
**Region**: EUR
**Delay**: 1

范围 EUR TOPCS1600 D1 REGULAR，MATRIX。S1 全量2332字段；本次仅用经描述与名称一致性核查的字段，排除 free_cash_flow（描述实际为 Other Financing Cash Flow）等语义冲突字段。缺完整分布包，明确降级为 metadata+经济含义约束，不能声称形状体检通过。
使用252日回填；季度财务变化用252日。每项字段精确绑定，不展开相似字段。禁止价格/模型字段、FCF-to-price饱和族、权重混合。原始量只在同一公司内部做比值，最终截面排名以降低规模影响。

**Concept**: Cash backing of accrued assets
**Mechanism**: More liquid cash relative to accrued operating assets reduces dependence on accounting accrual realization.
**Fields**: accrued_cash_equivalents, accrued_assets_total.
**Implementation Example**: `rank(divide(ts_backfill({accrued_cash_equivalents},252),ts_backfill({accrued_assets_total},252)))`

**Concept**: Trade receivable funding pressure
**Mechanism**: More trade receivables relative to available cash ties up liquidity. Prefer the negative ratio within industry, a working-capital quality hypothesis.
**Fields**: accrued_accounts_receivable, accrued_cash_equivalents.
**Implementation Example**: `reverse(rank(divide(ts_backfill({accrued_accounts_receivable},252),ts_backfill({accrued_cash_equivalents},252))))`

**Concept**: Supplier credit versus trade receivables
**Mechanism**: Trade payables can finance the receivables cycle. A larger payable-to-receivable ratio reduces external cash needs, provided it is not merely distress.
**Fields**: accounts_payable_4, accrued_accounts_receivable.
**Implementation Example**: `rank(divide(ts_backfill({accounts_payable_4},252),ts_backfill({accrued_accounts_receivable},252)))`

**Concept**: Financing dependence versus operating cash
**Mechanism**: Cash raised from financing relative to cash generated by operations measures external funding dependence. Higher dependence is hypothesized to predict weaker returns.
**Fields**: total_financing_cash_flow, operating_cashflow.
**Implementation Example**: `reverse(rank(divide(ts_backfill({total_financing_cash_flow},252),abs(ts_backfill({operating_cashflow},252)))))`

**Concept**: Cash conversion of after-tax income
**Mechanism**: Operating cash relative to net income measures earnings realization. Prefer earnings backed by cash instead of pure accruals.
**Fields**: operating_cashflow, total_income_after_taxes.
**Implementation Example**: `rank(divide(ts_backfill({operating_cashflow},252),abs(ts_backfill({total_income_after_taxes},252))))`

**Concept**: Interest accrual burden on liquidity
**Mechanism**: Accrued unpaid interest relative to cash reveals funding stress. Rank low burdens positively.
**Fields**: accrued_interest_payable, accrued_cash_equivalents.
**Implementation Example**: `reverse(rank(divide(ts_backfill({accrued_interest_payable},252),ts_backfill({accrued_cash_equivalents},252))))`

**Concept**: Inventory commitment versus receivables
**Mechanism**: Inventory that outgrows customer receivables may signal slowing sell-through. Test the balance-sheet composition rather than the level of company assets.
**Fields**: fnd23_tot_inventory, trade_receivables_current.
**Implementation Example**: `reverse(rank(divide(ts_backfill({fnd23_tot_inventory},252),ts_backfill({trade_receivables_current},252))))`

**Concept**: Deteriorating collection against cash buffer
**Mechanism**: A year-on-year rise in receivable-to-cash commitment is a deterioration in working-capital conversion; lower is better.
**Fields**: accounts_receivable_total_5, accrued_cash_equivalents.
**Implementation Example**: `reverse(rank(ts_delta(divide(ts_backfill({accounts_receivable_total_5},252),ts_backfill({accrued_cash_equivalents},252)),252)))`
